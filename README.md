# GigaDeviceSemiconductor
GigaDevice Semiconductor Inc. founded in April 2005, is a leading global fabless supplier dedicated to developing advanced memory technologies, microcontrollers (MCUs), sensor, analog products and solutions. Through operations and distributor networks across Singapore, China, the United States, South Korea, Japan, the United Kingdom, Germany, Turkey, Italy, and Mexico, GigaDevice can provide customers with prompt and reliable local support.

GigaDevice GD32 Microcontrollers (MCUs) boasts many years of industry-leading design experience, having already launched a wide variety of 32-bit general purpose microcontrollers based on Arm® Cortex®-M and RISC-V cores. GigaDevice continues to empower various industry fields, providing high-performance and resource-rich MCUs.
